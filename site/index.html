02Mint

The vault mints MDELTA in return.

MDELTA is your vault share: a token that represents a claim on the vault's net asset value. It is minted at the live price, so every depositor enters at a fair value.

shares=USDC in×supply÷total assets

Hold it, transfer it, or redeem it for USDC at NAV.

03Inside the vault

Now look inside.

Your USDC goes to work in one market-neutral trade, the cash & carry. It earns yield without betting on where ETH goes next.

04Buy spot

Buy spot ETH.

Holding ETH alone means full exposure to its price. That's a delta of +1. On its own, it tips the scale.

Spot ETH, Δ +190.91% of capital
05Short the perp

Short an equal ETH perpetual.

A short perpetual future of exactly the same size cancels the price exposure: +1 − 1 = 0. The scale levels out. The vault is delta-neutral.

ETH-PERP short, Δ −19.09% margin at 10×
06Delta 0

ETH moves. The scale doesn't.

If ETH rallies, the spot leg gains what the short loses. If it crashes, the short gains what spot loses. Net price exposure stays at zero.

07Collect the carry

Get paid to hold the hedge.

Perpetual futures pay a funding rate. When the market leans long, longs pay shorts. The vault collects that payment every hour and compounds it into NAV, so each MDELTA is worth more over time.

09Security

How we keep it sound.

  1. 01

    Delta-neutral by design

    Spot and short are sized equally. A keeper rebalances and reinvests funding every hour.

  2. 02

    Non-custodial vault

    Assets sit in an ERC-4626 contract, not with us. Mint and redeem at live NAV.

  3. 03

    5% liquidity buffer

    About 5% of NAV stays in USDC to pay redemptions instantly.

  4. 04

    Fair exits

    Pro-rata partial fills, and an exit fee only on net outflows that stays in the vault for remaining holders.

  5. 05

    Fair fees, ERC-8113

    No management fee. The performance fee applies only above each deposit's own high-water mark.

  6. 06

    Audit before mainnet

    Formal verification, a tier-1 audit, multisig and timelock admin, and an optional KYC whitelist.
