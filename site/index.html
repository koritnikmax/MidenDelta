04Strategy 01: cash & carry

Buy spot ETH.

Holding ETH means full exposure to its price. That is a delta of +1. On its own, it tips the scale.

Spot ETH, Δ +1
05Short the perp

Short an equal ETH perpetual.

A short perpetual future of exactly the same size cancels the price exposure: +1 − 1 = 0. The scale levels out. The position is delta-neutral.

ETH-PERP short, Δ −1Same size as spot
06Delta 0

ETH moves. The scale doesn't.

If ETH rallies, the spot leg gains what the short loses. If it crashes, the short gains what spot loses. Net price exposure stays close to zero.

07Collect the carry

Get paid to hold the hedge.

Perpetual futures pay a funding rate. When more traders are long than short, longs pay shorts, on Hyperliquid every hour. The short leg collects it. Funding moves with the market and can turn negative, so we also cover when the trade stops being worth it.
