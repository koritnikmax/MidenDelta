01Subscribe

Your USDC waits in the fund's vault contract until the next dealing day. Subscriptions and redemptions settle daily at the NAV struck by the fund administrator.

02Issue

The fund issues units to you.

MDELTA units are ERC-3643 tokens: the fund's unit register, on-chain. They are issued at the administrator's NAV, so every investor enters at a fair value.

units=USDC in÷NAV per unit

Units move only between verified, eligible investors and are redeemed at NAV on dealing days.

03Inside the vault

Now look inside.

Your USDC goes to work in one market-neutral trade, the cash & carry. It earns yield without betting on where ETH goes next.

04Buy spot

Buy spot ETH.

Holding ETH alone means full exposure to its price. That's a delta of +1. On its own, it tips the scale.

Spot ETH, Δ +171% of NAV in spot
05Short the perp

Short an equal ETH perpetual.

A short perpetual future of exactly the same size cancels the price exposure: +1 − 1 = 0. The scale levels out. The vault is delta-neutral.

ETH-PERP short, Δ −1Leverage capped at 3×
06Delta 0

ETH moves. The scale doesn't.

If ETH rallies, the spot leg gains what the short loses. If it crashes, the short gains what spot loses. Net price exposure stays close to zero.

07Collect the carry

Get paid to hold the hedge.

Perpetual futures pay a funding rate. When the market leans long, longs pay shorts. The fund collects that payment every hour and reinvests it, so it accrues to the NAV. Funding varies and can turn negative.

09Security

How we keep it sound.

  1. 01

    Delta-neutral, leverage capped at 3×

    Spot and short are sized equally. A keeper re-hedges when delta drifts and tops up margin from a 5% buffer.

  2. 02

    Supervised by a licensed AIFM

    The strategy runs on-chain; a licensed Liechtenstein fund manager sets its limits, owns risk and holds the kill switch.

  3. 03

    Permissioned units

    ERC-3643 units that only KYC-verified professional and semi-professional investors can hold. No US persons.

  4. 04

    Controlled keys and breakers

    Admin changes go through the manager's multisig and a timelock. The keeper executes but can't withdraw, and circuit breakers pause the fund.

  5. 05

    Orderly dealing

    Daily dealing at NAV, a redemption gate with pro-rata fills, and an anti-dilution levy that stays in the fund.

  6. 06

    Audited, then multi-venue

    Formal verification and a tier-1 audit before mainnet. Fully on Hyperliquid today; more venues later to spread platform risk.

Structure

On-chain strategy, supervised by an AIFM.

Vault 01 is being set up as a Liechtenstein alternative investment fund. The strategy runs on-chain, and a licensed fund manager (AIFM) supervises it: it sets the limits, owns risk management and answers to the regulator. The smart contracts enforce who can do what. Over time, MidenDelta plans to obtain its own AIFM licence in Liechtenstein.
