Diligence

Questions allocators ask.

What exactly do I hold?

MDELTA, an ERC-20 share token of an ERC-4626 vault. Each share is a pro-rata claim on the vault's net asset value. It is minted when you deposit USDC and burned when you redeem.

Where does the return come from?

Mainly from perpetual-futures funding. When the market is net long, longs pay shorts, and the vault holds the short leg. Spot ETH offsets the price exposure, so the position is delta-neutral. Funding rates vary and can turn negative, and that is the main source of return variance.

What if many holders redeem at once?

About 5% of NAV is held idle in USDC for instant redemptions. Larger exits are queued and filled pro-rata. A dynamic exit fee applies only to net outflows, and it stays in the vault for the holders who remain.

How are fees charged?

There is no management fee. A 19.5% performance fee applies only to gains above each deposit's own high-water mark (ERC-8113). The first $10M of deposits pay no performance fee during year one.

Is it audited?

Not yet. Vault 01 runs today as a testnet prototype. Before mainnet we will complete formal verification, a tier-1 audit, multisig and timelock controls on admin roles, and a full legal review.
