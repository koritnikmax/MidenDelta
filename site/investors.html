Questions allocators ask.

Who can invest?

Professional investors, and semi-professional investors who invest at least €200,000 and meet the other statutory conditions. US persons and residents of sanctioned countries are excluded. Every investor is onboarded and KYC-checked by the fund administrator before their wallet can hold units.

What exactly do I hold?

MDELTA fund units: ERC-3643 permissioned tokens that form the fund's unit register on-chain. They are issued when a subscription settles and burned when a redemption settles, always at the administrator's NAV. Units can only be held by verified, eligible wallets.

How does dealing work?

You request a subscription in USDC or a redemption in units at any time. At the daily cutoff the dealing period closes, the administrator strikes the NAV, and requests settle at that NAV. You then claim your units or USDC. There is no instant mint or redeem.

Where does the return come from?

Mainly from perpetual-futures funding. When the market is net long, longs pay shorts, and the fund holds the short leg. Spot ETH offsets the price exposure. Funding rates vary and can turn negative, which is the main source of return variance. Returns can be negative.

What if many investors redeem at once?

If net redemptions on a dealing day exceed 15% of NAV, every request is filled by the same percentage and the rest rolls to the next dealing day with no priority. Net outflows pay an anti-dilution levy that stays in the fund for the remaining investors.

How are fees charged?

There is no management fee. A 19.5% performance fee applies only to gains above the high-water mark, calculated by the fund administrator. Fund costs such as administration, depositary and audit are charged to the fund. Founding investors in the first $10M pay no performance fee in year one, subject to the final fund documents.

Who controls the fund?

The strategy runs on-chain and is supervised by a licensed Liechtenstein AIFM. The AIFM sets the limits the strategy runs within, owns risk management and valuation oversight, holds the admin keys through a multisig and timelock, and decides when to resume after a circuit breaker. A depositary oversees the assets. MidenDelta builds and operates the strategy under the AIFM's mandate; its execution keeper cannot withdraw assets or change limits. Over time, MidenDelta plans to obtain its own AIFM licence in Liechtenstein and manage the funds directly.

Is it audited?

Not yet. Vault 01 runs today as a testnet prototype. Before mainnet we will complete formal verification, a tier-1 audit and a full legal review with the fund manager.
